About GUKIN

Built from the gut out.

Most supplement ranges treat gut, skin and energy as three separate shelves. They are one pathway. GUKIN was built to support it end to end, at clinical dose, with nothing hidden behind a blend.

Why we started

The question that started GUKIN was narrow: why do bloating, dull skin and flat afternoons so often arrive together? The answer, repeatedly, was upstream. A compromised intestinal barrier lets bacterial fragments into circulation. The low-grade inflammation that follows suppresses collagen synthesis, disrupts sleep architecture and drags on cellular energy. Three symptoms, one cause.

Almost nothing on the shelf was built for that. Gut products ignored skin. Skin products ignored the gut. Longevity products sold a single molecule as though it worked in isolation. So we built the range the way the biology actually runs — as a sequence, from absorption through to healthspan.

What functional longevity means

Lifespan is how long you live. Healthspan is how long you live well — without meaningful decline in energy, mobility, cognition or appearance. Across most populations the gap between the two runs to more than a decade.

Functional longevity is the work of closing that gap. Not chasing a number, but keeping the systems that decide how the next thirty years feel in good repair: barrier integrity, inflammatory tone, NAD+ availability, sleep quality, collagen turnover.

How we formulate

01 · Dose

Clinical dose, or nothing

An active at a fraction of its studied dose is decoration. Every ingredient in the range appears at the amount the research used, or it does not appear.

02 · Form

The form the body can use

Magnesium glycinate rather than oxide. Zinc L-carnosine rather than generic zinc. Bioavailability is not a detail — it is most of the result.

03 · Transparency

No proprietary blends

A blend is a way of hiding how little of something is present. Every label names each active and its milligram dose. Batch certificates are available on request.

04 · Testing

Third-party verified

Manufactured in GMP-certified facilities. Every batch is independently tested for identity, potency and heavy metals before it ships.

05 · Certification

Halal, always

Every formulation is halal-certified and free of porcine gelatin and alcohol. The gummies use pectin, which also makes them suitable for vegetarians and vegans.

06 · Climate

Built for this heat

Stability-tested to 40°C and packed with desiccant. A formula that degrades in a Karachi summer is not a formula, whatever the label claims.

Where we are

GUKIN operates from Karachi and Dubai, shipping across Pakistan and the UAE from local fulfilment, and internationally beyond that. Local warehousing is why delivery is fast and duty-paid in both markets rather than a three-week customs wait.

The brand is global in standard and regional in service. Formulated to international specification, priced and delivered for the people actually buying it.

What we will not claim

No supplement stops ageing, and we will not tell you otherwise. What the evidence supports is narrower and still worth having: correcting a deficiency, restoring a declining precursor, reducing chronic inflammation. Sleep, protein, resistance training and sun protection remain the largest levers available to you. These formulations are built to support that work, not to replace it.
